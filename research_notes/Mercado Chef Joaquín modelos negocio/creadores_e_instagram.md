# Spanish-speaking creator/infoproduct economy and Instagram growth mechanics (for "Chef Joaquín", private/yacht chef)

Research date: 2026-11-18. Method note: direct page fetching was blocked in this session (hosts such as metricool.com, socialmediatoday.com, que.es and dplnews.com did not resolve), so findings come from search-result summaries of the cited pages. Figures should be re-checked against the primary page before publication. Vendor-reported numbers are flagged as such.

## 1. Selling infoproducts and mentorships to Spanish-speaking audiences: platforms, prices, payment methods, product ladder

### Takeaway
Hispanic LatAm is a fast-growing, professionalising creator market (Hotmart study, Jan 2026), led by Colombia, Mexico, Argentina and Peru. More than half of LatAm producer sales cross borders, and local payment methods (OXXO, PSE, Mercado Pago, SEPA/SeQura in Spain) are standard on Hotmart. I found no reliable public data on average ticket, conversion rate or purchasing-power price tiers per country. Those numbers have to come from Joaquín's own tests.

### Cited Findings
**Market size and structure (Hotmart, "Economía de Creadores en América Latina Hispanohablante", published Jan 2026)**
- Colombia is the top country of origin for Hotmart producers in Hispanic LatAm, followed by Mexico, Argentina and Peru — [Fast Company MX, 28 Jan 2026](https://fastcompany.mx/2026/01/28/creator-economy-america-latina-industria-global/); [Semana](https://www.semana.com/economia/macroeconomia/articulo/colombia-lidera-la-economia-de-creadores-en-la-region/202612/); [Revista PyM](https://www.revistapym.com.co/articulos/comunicacion/94067/colombia-lidera-la-economia-de-creadores-en-hispanoamerica)

**Product-ladder signals (Kajabi data, 2025)**
- Kajabi "State of Creator Commerce" (April 2025, n=1,717): about 40% of new products on Kajabi are non-course offers (coaching, communities, memberships). The 4.5× figure comes from a secondary write-up and was not confirmed in the primary report — [Lindsey Gamble on Kajabi data](https://www.lindseygamble.com/blog/new-data-from-kajabi-reveals-how-creators-are-finding-success-in-2025); [Kajabi report page](https://kajabi.com/state-of-the-creator-economy); [Sacra analysis](https://sacra.com/chat/h/050013e0-8d9a-4bab-85e3-99a6cab13e9f/)

### Inferences
- Platform fit for Joaquín: Hotmart for LatAm low and mid ticket (local cash and bank methods, instalments, an affiliate network). Stan Store or Gumroad for a fast link-in-bio low-ticket product. Skool or Circle for a paid community, and a direct application plus call for high-ticket mentoring.
- Typical Spanish-language ladder seen in the market (my synthesis, not a measured benchmark): free lead magnet (PDF guide or checklist, mini-class) → low ticket €/USD 7–47 (recipe system, "first private-chef client" guide, yacht-chef CV/interview kit) → mid ticket €/USD 97–497 (course or cohort) → community subscription (USD 20–100/month) → high-ticket mentoring €/USD 1,500–6,000+.
- Regional pricing: since Hotmart does not appear to offer automatic purchasing-power pricing, the practical move is separate offers or coupons per currency, with EUR prices for Spain and the yacht or international audience and lower USD/MXN/COP prices for LatAm. Joaquín's yacht-chef career niche likely has a higher-income buyer (crew paid in EUR/USD), which supports EUR pricing.

### Gaps
- No public data found on average ticket price or checkout conversion by country (Spain vs. Mexico vs. Colombia vs. Argentina) for Hotmart or the other platforms. The full Hotmart study could not be retrieved.
- No official Hotmart page on purchasing-power-parity pricing was found.
- No Spain-specific creator-economy dataset was found (the Hotmart study covers Hispanic LatAm).

## 2. Funnel benchmarks: follower→lead, lead→buyer, ManyChat keyword funnels, email/WhatsApp lists

### Takeaway
Comment-keyword→DM automation (ManyChat) is the dominant Instagram lead-capture mechanic. Vendor case studies show strong results (2%→8% comment-to-lead, 35% purchase conversion on warm audiences), but there is no independent standard benchmark. General opt-in benchmarks are about 2% average and 3–8% when well optimised.

### Cited Findings
- Jenna Kutcher (ManyChat case study, vendor-reported): a comment-keyword post sent the registration link by DM, collecting 2,650 emails and 924 purchases, described as a "35% purchase conversion rate" — [ManyChat case study via casestudies.com](https://www.casestudies.com/company/manychat/case-study/jenna-kutcher-drives-17m-in-sales-with-manychat)
- Amy Porterfield-type case (vendor): "85% of people who commented ended up registering", with USD 1.4M revenue over 10 months. Planoly flags this as vendor data — [Planoly](https://www.planoly.com/blog/do-automated-instagram-dms-work)
- Mindvalley: +522% masterclass sign-ups with Instagram automation (vendor) — [casestudies.com](https://www.casestudies.com/company/manychat/case-study/how-mindvalley-achieved-a-522-increase-in-masterclass-sign-ups-using-instagram-automation); Epic Fab Girl: +118% leads compared with landing-page opt-ins — [casestudies.com](https://www.casestudies.com/company/manychat/case-study/how-candace-junee-increased-leads-by-118-with-instagram-automation)
- EZContacts: comment-to-lead rate grew from 2% to 8% after adding comment-to-DM flows (vendor) — [Planoly summary](https://www.planoly.com/blog/do-automated-instagram-dms-work)
- "There's no standard benchmark, because vendors define conversion differently." Planoly recommends comparing against your own link-in-bio results — [Planoly](https://www.planoly.com/blog/do-automated-instagram-dms-work)
- DM delivery is not guaranteed: one dataset shows 92.25% delivered (52,633 of 57,052 attempts) — [Planoly](https://www.planoly.com/blog/do-automated-instagram-dms-work)
- Claims of "80–100% / 90% DM open rates" come from unsourced affiliate posts — [unkoa](https://www.unkoa.com/instagram-dm-automation-vs-email-in-2025-why-manychat-delivers-90-open-rates-and-60-reply-rates/)
- ManyChat's guide for course creators — [ManyChat blog](https://manychat.com/blog/how-course-creators-use-instagram-dm-automation/)
- Opt-in benchmarks (2026, vendor/aggregator): average email opt-in 2.1%. Under 1.5% is below average, 1.5–3% average, 3–8% well optimised, 8%+ top performers. Dedicated landing pages run 12–25% compared with 4–8% for pop-ups (discount magnet example). Healthy mature-list growth is 1–3% per month — [shno.co](https://www.shno.co/marketing-statistics/lead-magnet-conversion-statistics); [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/resources/lead-magnet-conversion-benchmark-cheat-sheet/); [focus-digital](https://focus-digital.co/lead-magnet-conversion-rate-by-industry/)

### Inferences
- A workable planning model for Joaquín (assumption, to validate): each reel or carousel carries one keyword CTA ("YATE", "MENU", "CV"). ManyChat sends the lead magnet and asks for email and/or WhatsApp. A welcome sequence then offers the low ticket. Plan around a few percent of leads buying the low ticket, and roughly 1 in 100 leads (or fewer) applying for high-ticket mentoring. These are conservative placeholders, not benchmarks.
- WhatsApp (lists or Channels) matters more in LatAm/Spain than email. No numeric benchmark was found (see Gaps).

### Gaps
- No independent study gives a follower→lead or lead→buyer rate for Spanish-language creators.
- No WhatsApp-list conversion benchmarks were found.
- No creator-webinar or launch conversion benchmarks were found.

## 3. Instagram algorithm, formats and 2025–2026 platform changes (official guidance where available)

### Takeaway
Instagram has said its top ranking signals are watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach (Mosseri, Jan 2025). Sends weigh more for reaching non-followers, and originality is an explicit ranking principle. In April 2026 the originality and anti-aggregator rule was extended from Reels to photos and carousels, applied account-wide over 30 days. Mosseri's year-end letter (late Dec 2025/Jan 2026) says raw, real, imperfect human content will gain value as AI floods feeds. Data from Metricool and Buffer support a mix of Reels for reach and carousels for saves and depth, posted frequently. Daily posting sits in the highest-growth band.

### Cited Findings
**Ranking signals (Mosseri, January 2025)**
- Watch time, likes and sends are the most important signals. Creators should track average watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach in Insights — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/); [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2-/instagram-reveals-key-factors-for-boosting-reach-8646916)
- Likes count slightly more for reaching followers, and sends (DM shares) count slightly more for reaching non-followers. Originality is the first of Instagram's stated ranking principles — [Buffer, 2026 guide](https://buffer.com/resources//instagram-algorithms/)
- Blog claims that "sends are weighted 3–5× likes" are not confirmed by Instagram — [search summary of SEO blogs, e.g. dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers)
- Instagram's Reels Insights include "View Rate", the percentage of viewers who keep watching past the first 3 seconds (official metric, so the hook window is real). Claims such as "60% retention target" or "+40% reach for early hooks" are unsourced — [Lindsey Gamble on new Reels insights](https://www.lindseygamble.com/blog/instagram-new-insights-for-reels); [inro.social](https://www.inro.social/blog/instagram-reels-3-second-hook-leads)

**Originality and reposts**
- 2024–2025: when Instagram detects identical content, it replaces reposted Reels with the original in recommendations. Penalties are account-level, and accounts regain eligibility once most of their posts over 30 days are original — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/instagrams-algorithm-overhaul-will-reward-original-content-and-penalize-aggregators-130018977.html); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-updates-algorithm-to-benefit-original-creators/819016/)
- April 30, 2026: Mosseri said the policy now also covers photos and carousels. Accounts that mainly post content they did not create, or did not meaningfully transform, are no longer recommended to non-followers. Evaluation is account-wide over about a month. Watermarks, minor crops or plain reposts do not count as original, while commentary, remixing and reinterpretation do. Followers can still see these accounts' posts. The change was live by about May 2, 2026 — [PetaPixel, 30 Apr 2026](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/); [Tubefilter, 30 Apr 2026](https://tubefilter.com/2026/04/30/instagram-removes-algorithm-recommendations-repost-content-aggregator); [Planoly](https://planoly.com/blog/instagram-updates-its-original-content-policy)
- The "10+ unoriginal posts in 30 days" threshold and "up to 3× reach for originals" figures come from a single secondary source quoting unnamed insiders. Unverified — [WebProNews](https://www.webpronews.com/instagrams-aggregator-purge-original-creators-gain-ground-as-repost-empires-crumble/)

**Authenticity in the AI era (Mosseri's year-end letter on Threads, published around Dec 31, 2025 to early Jan 2026)**
- "Authenticity is becoming infinitely reproducible", and "the feeds are starting to fill up with synthetic everything." He expects too-polished content to lose appeal, attention to shift to raw, real, imperfect content, and Instagram to label AI content and "fingerprint real media". He also wrote: "Authenticity is fast becoming a scarce resource, which will in turn drive more demand for creator content, not less" — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/social-media/instagram-chief-ai-is-so-ubiquitous-it-will-be-more-practical-to-fingerprint-real-media-than-fake-media-202620080.html); [Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/general/40060637); [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/instagram-says-ai-killed-the-curated-feed-now-its-scrambling-to-prove-whats-real); [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/ai-platforms-assistants/ai-slop-won-in-2025-fingerprinting-real-content-might-be-the-answer-in-2026)

**Format and tool changes**
- January 2025: Reels can run up to 3 minutes (previously 90 s). The profile grid changed to vertical rectangles. Edits, a standalone video editor that competes with CapCut and offers collaborative drafts and insights, was announced and expected around March 13, 2025 — [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-introduces-three-minute-reels-8636683); [Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/instagram-major-updates-new-features); [Netinfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/instagram-announces-3-minute-reels-and-vertical-grids/)
- Trial Reels launched in December 2024 and are shown only to non-followers first. Access widened in 2025, scheduling was added, and Instagram said in June 2025 that 40% of creators who used Trial Reels posted more often — [Business Standard](https://business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/instagram-introduces-trial-reels-feature-what-it-is-how-it-works-more-124121100429_1.html); [BetaNews, Jul 2025](https://betanews.com/2025/07/14/instagram-opens-up-trial-reels-feature-to-more-creators/); [The Week, Jun 2025](https://www.theweek.in/news/sci-tech/2025/06/24/trial-reels-quiet-posting-and-grid-control-what-instagram-s-2025-update-means-for-you.html); [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-introduces-wider-access-trial-reels-9493132)
- Since April 2025, Instagram uses "views" instead of impressions as the common metric across formats, which affects year-on-year comparisons — [Metricool study 2026 coverage, Reason Why](https://www.reasonwhy.es/actualidad/estudio-instagram-metricool-2026)

**Format performance data (Metricool, a Spanish company)**
- Metricool Instagram study: average reach per post is 14,918 for Reels, 11,573 for carousels and 4,779 for single images. Reels average 1,035 interactions. Metricool recommends Reels for reach and carousels for depth — [Metricool (ES)](https://metricool.com/es/instagram-reels-vs-post-vs-carrusel/)
- Metricool Instagram Study 2026 (Jan–Feb 2025 compared with Jan–Feb 2026): carousels get 9× more saves than single images, more than double the reach and views, and almost 4× the interactions. Reels grew in shares (+67%), interactions (+32%), likes (+18%) and reach (+12%). Average engagement rate fell from 3.79% to 3.54% (−7%). Coverage headlines: "more Reels, fewer hashtags, and only three days to stand out" (a post's useful life is about 3 days) — [Metricool tendencias 2026](https://metricool.com/es/tendencias-instagram/); [Metricool press release](https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-study-2026/); [marketing4ecommerce MX](https://marketing4ecommerce.mx/que-funciona-en-instagram-en-2026/); [Reason Why](https://www.reasonwhy.es/actualidad/estudio-instagram-metricool-2026)
- Metricool × HypeAuditor Content Playbook 2025 (Jan–Jun 2025, six account-size tiers): the better format depends on account size, and carousels outperform Reels for larger accounts — [Social Media Today press release](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/press-release/20251010-metricool-and-hypeauditor-unveil-2025-instagram-content-playbook-global-be); [Netinfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/instagram-carousels-outperform-reels-for-larger-accounts-content-performance-benchmarks-show/); [Lia Haberman](https://liahaberman.substack.com/p/the-best-instagram-content-formats)
- Conflicting coverage: Merca2.0's summary of Metricool's 2025 study said Reels reach declined while carousels (up to 20 images) gained — [Merca2.0](https://www.merca20.com/?p=12474025); [Marketing Directo](https://www.marketingdirecto.com/imprescindibles/social-media/carruseles-destronan-resto-formatos-cambian-reglas-instagram)

**Posting frequency (Buffer, Aug 13, 2025, about 2.1M posts from about 102K accounts)**
- Compared with 1–2 posts/week, reach per post was about 12% higher at 3–5/week, about 18% higher at 6–9/week and about 24% higher at 10+/week. Monthly follower-growth gain was +0.12% at 1–2/week, +0.26% at 3–5, +0.44% at 6–9 and +0.66% at 10+. Weeks with no posts show a "no-posting penalty" — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/); [Social Media Today](https://socialmediatoday.com/news/study-shows-posting-more-instagram-leads-to-more-reach/757633). A NetInfluencer summary contradicts this, saying reach per post declines with frequency — [Netinfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/replying-to-comments-boosts-social-media-engagement-across-all-major-platforms-data-shows/)
- Replying to comments is associated with higher engagement across platforms (Buffer data) — [Netinfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/replying-to-comments-boosts-social-media-engagement-across-all-major-platforms-data-shows/)

### Inferences
- One post per day (7/week) puts Joaquín in Buffer's 6–9/week band, the second-highest growth tier, and well above most accounts. The main risk is quality dropping, not the algorithm.
- Suggested weekly mix based on the data: 4–5 Reels (reach and sends: yacht-galley POV, "what a yacht chef earns", provisioning in port) and 2–3 carousels (saves: menus, provisioning checklists, CV and interview tips). Use Trial Reels to test hooks on non-followers without cluttering the grid.
- Optimise for sends ("send this to your friend who wants to work on yachts") and saves (checklists). Answer comments quickly, since keyword comments also feed engagement.
- Originality rules favour Joaquín's real galley and yacht footage. Never repost others' food clips. AI-polished or stock visuals work against Mosseri's "raw and real" direction.
- Length: up to 3 min is allowed, but no official optimal length exists. Keep most Reels short (roughly 15–45 s) with a strong first 3 s (View Rate), and use longer ones for storytelling. This is a recommendation, not a sourced fact.

### Gaps
- No official or credible source was found for a "3-hour originality rule". This may be a misremembered claim (possibly about Metricool's "3 days" post lifespan). Treat it as unverified.
- No 2025–2026 official statement was found on Broadcast Channels' effect on reach. Broadcast Channels exist as a one-to-many DM tool for followers, but no performance data was retrieved.
- No official optimal Reel length was found, and Mosseri's exact original wording on the signals could not be retrieved (fetching was blocked).

## 4. What works for chef/food/career-coaching accounts; content pillars

### Takeaway
Chef-creators who monetise well combine a real professional credential (Michelin, celebrity or private chef), multi-platform content and a mix of income streams (private dining, memberships, brand deals, digital products). The paid-education niche for chefs exists but is small and early. A Skool search shows only a few small chef communities, which is an opening for a yacht/private-chef career offer.

### Cited Findings
- Chef Bae is a celebrity private chef with 1.5M+ followers across YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, plus product collaborations and a cookbook. Her model keeps "the kitchen at the center" of the business — [Netinfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/creator-chef-founder-why-chef-bae-keeps-the-kitchen-at-the-center-of-her-business/)
- Food Blogger Pro features a former Michelin chef turned private and celebrity chef who now builds a content business — [Food Blogger Pro](https://www.foodbloggerpro.com/449/)
- Anecdotal TikTok claim: some private chefs earn as much or more from partnerships as from cheffing — [Passes](https://www.passes.com/blog/posts/how-to-make-money-as-a-chef)
- Escoffier's guide on building a food-content career across TikTok, Instagram and YouTube — [Escoffier](https://www.escoffier.edu/blog/food-entrepreneurship/food-content-creator-tiktok-instagram-youtube/)
- Chef-niche paid communities exist but are small: Chef's Harvest Inner Circle (USD 6,000, 5 members) and Cooking Creators (8 reviews) — [Skool](https://www.skool.com/chefs-harvest-inner-circle-8941); [Skool](https://www.skool.com/cookingcreators)
- Hotmart data shows creators can grow below 50K followers (74% of growing creators), so audience size is not the bottleneck — [que.es](https://www.que.es/2026/06/25/74-por-ciento-creadores-menos-50-mil-seguidores-hotmart/)

### Inferences (content pillars, my synthesis of common expert-brand frameworks; no single authoritative source was retrieved)
- Authority/proof (about 20%): galley service, menus for owners and guests, credentials, client results ("how X got her first yacht job").
- Behind the scenes (about 25%): life on board, provisioning in Antibes/Palma/Caribbean, crew mess, schedules. This is the strongest original, hard-to-copy content and fits the anti-aggregator and "raw" rules.
- Educational (about 25%): techniques, provisioning checklists, allergy management, career steps (STCW, CV, agencies, day-rates). Carousels drive saves.
- Storytelling (about 15%): Joaquín's path, mistakes, owners' anecdotes (anonymised), the cost of the lifestyle.
- Offer/CTA (about 10–15%): keyword comment → lead magnet, low-ticket launches, mentoring applications.
- The "career-coaching for chefs → private/yacht chef" angle probably monetises better than recipes (recipes are commoditised by AI and free content), because it sells a career and income outcome.

### Gaps
- No Spanish-language data was found on chef or yacht-chef creator monetisation or audience size, and no benchmark study exists for food or career-coaching accounts specifically.
- No primary source was found for a standard "content pillar" framework with percentages. The splits above are inference.

## 5. AI's impact on the infoproduct market, saturation, skepticism and trust

### Takeaway
Generic information is being commoditised by free content and AI (90–95% of Hotmart creators already use AI). Platforms and creators are shifting from courses to communities, coaching and memberships (about 40% of new Kajabi products are non-course). In Spanish-speaking markets, "vende humo" distrust of coaches is a recognised barrier, and trust comes from verifiable real-world expertise, raw behind-the-scenes proof and access to a person.

### Inferences
- For Joaquín, a "recipes course" competes with ChatGPT and YouTube, while "become a yacht/private chef" mentoring, with real contacts, CV review, interview prep, a crew-agency network and accountability, is defensible against AI.
- Trust builders to emphasise: verifiable career facts (yachts, seasons, owners anonymised), unpolished on-board footage, specific numbers (day rates, tips, contracts), student outcomes with names and permission, low-risk entry products, and free value before the pitch. Avoid "lifestyle/Lambo" coach aesthetics and income claims without proof, which trigger "vende humo" skepticism.
- AI should be used behind the scenes (scripts, captions, repurposing) rather than in visible content, in line with Instagram's labelling and "real media" direction.
